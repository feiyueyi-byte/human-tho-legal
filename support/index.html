Common questions

How do I record a journal entry?

Open the Journal tab and tap the record button. Talk for as long as you'd like, then stop — you'll get a chance to rate your day and save it.

My entry didn't turn into text automatically — is that normal?

Automatic transcription isn't available in every build yet. If it doesn't appear, you can always type your entry directly into the text box that shows up after recording — the audio itself is still saved either way.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Recordings, entries, and your streak all live locally — see the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

How do I delete an entry?

Open the entry from your journal list, then use the delete option there. This removes it and its recording from your device.

What is Our Planet?

A place to share one small, ordinary thing anonymously, and read what other people are noticing right now. Anything you share is visible for 24 hours unless you choose to keep it.

Something's broken — what should I include when I email you?

What you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead, and your device model + iOS version if you have it. Screenshots help a lot.
