Privacy Policy

Human, tho

Last updated September 30, 2026

Short version: your voice stays on your phone, your location is optional and never shown to anyone, and we don't have accounts, ads, or analytics to sell you out to in the first place.

What the app collects

Voice recordings

When you record a journal entry, the audio is saved on your device only. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or seen by us.

Location

When you choose to share a glimpse on Our Planet, you can optionally share your location so it can be placed on the map. This is entirely optional — you can share a general area instead of a precise one, and either way, no address or exact coordinates are ever shown to other people. It's used only to place your light.

Journal entries

Your written and recorded entries, mood scores, and streak history are stored locally on your device using standard on-device storage. We don't have a server that holds a copy.

What we don't do

—No account or sign-in — there's nothing tying your entries to your identity on our end
—No analytics or tracking SDKs
—No advertising, and no ad networks
—We never sell or share your data with third parties

Our Planet

Glimpses you share on Our Planet are anonymous — shown with a general place name, never your name or exact location. Shared glimpses are visible to other people using the app for 24 hours, then removed, unless you choose to save one to your own memories.

Your control

You can delete any journal entry, or any saved memory, at any time from within the app. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.

Children's privacy

Human, tho is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
